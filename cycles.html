Notes

Data: month-end closes. Historical cycles are frozen and never re-fetched — the 2012 cycle uses Bitstamp closes (Nov 2012–Jun 2016, frozen Nov 7, 2026); the 2016 and 2020 cycles and everything from Sep 2014 on use Yahoo Finance BTC-USD. Only the 2024 cycle updates, daily: the current month shows the live Coinbase price until the month ends and locks.

Cycles: each runs from its halving month to the month before the next halving. Halvings: Nov 28, 2012 · Jul 9, 2016 · May 11, 2020 · Apr 19, 2024. The 2024 line is scaled to its range so far, so a new high or low rescales it. Because each cycle uses its own range, 100% is a different gain per cycle (~90× in 2012, ~25× in 2016, ~8× in 2020) — the cards above give each cycle’s dollar range.
