Network & supply clock

Bitcoin's issuance runs on a fixed schedule: every 210,000 blocks the new-coin reward per block halves, until the total reaches just under 21 million. Blocks arrive about every ten minutes, so the schedule is measured in blocks and the calendar dates are estimates.

Notes

Supply is computed from the subsidy schedule (50 BTC per block, halving every 210,000 blocks, in whole satoshis). It slightly overstates spendable coins: the genesis reward and a few miner under-claims are unspendable.

Halving date estimate assumes exactly ten-minute blocks from the current tip. In practice blocks have run a little faster than that while hashrate grows, so the halving has typically arrived a few weeks early.
