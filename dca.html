A little, on a schedule

Dollar-cost averaging means buying the same dollar amount at regular intervals, whatever the price. You buy more bitcoin when it's cheap and less when it's dear, and you never have to pick the right day. Set a plan below to see how it would have gone.

Your average cost against the price

Average cost is total dollars paid divided by bitcoin bought. Whenever the price is above that line the plan is in profit. Steady buying drags the line toward the price, so dips pull your cost down.

What if you had started at the very top?

The same plan, begun at the top of each bull run (the highest close in the two years after a halving), the worst possible day to start. "All at once" puts the same total in on that first day instead.

Notes

Purchases are made at the daily close (UTC) on each scheduled day; monthly plans buy on the same day of the month as the start date. Exchange fees, spreads and taxes aren't included, and would lower these results a little. The latest value uses the live price.

This is a look back at history, not a forecast or a recommendation.
