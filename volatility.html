How wild is the ride?

Volatility is how much the price jumps around from one day to the next. This page states it as a typical daily swing: if it reads ±3%, an ordinary day moves the price about 3% up or down, and bigger days are less common.

typical daily swing = standard deviation of daily % changes

Notes

Daily changes compare one UTC daily close with the next, from Nov 28, 2012 through the last completed day. The typical swing is the standard deviation of those changes over the stated window; multiply by about 19 (the square root of 365) for the annualized figure analysts quote. Early-2013 prices come from a single, thinly traded exchange, which exaggerates some of the largest moves.
