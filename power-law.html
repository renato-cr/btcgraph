Where the trend line goes

The fitted line evaluated on future dates. This is arithmetic on a curve fitted to the past, not a forecast: the model has no mechanism that forces price back to it.

Method

Fit: ordinary least squares of log₁₀(price) on log₁₀(days since Jan 3, 2009), over every month-end close from November 2012. Each close is dated to the last day of its month. σ is the standard deviation of the residuals in log₁₀ units, so +1σ means the price is 10^σ times the trend.

Caveat: the fit is refreshed whenever the monthly history updates, so the line itself moves slightly over time. Power-law fits are sensitive to the start date; excluding pre-2012 data (as here) gives a slightly shallower slope than fits that start in 2010.
