Cheap or expensive?

A price alone says little. Compare it with the 200-day average, roughly what buyers paid over the past seven months, and you get one number: above 1× bitcoin costs more than its recent average, below 1× it costs less.

multiple = price ÷ average close of the last 200 days

Notes

The price-to-200-day-average ratio is often called the Mayer Multiple, after Trace Mayer. Daily closes run from the first halving (Nov 28, 2012), so the 200-day average starts in mid-2013 and the 200-week average in late 2016. Today's value uses the live price.

"A year later" compares each day's close with the close 365 days on, so the most recent year isn't counted yet. Past results describe history, not what happens next.
