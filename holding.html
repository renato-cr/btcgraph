Notes

Based on daily closes from Nov 28, 2012 through the last completed day. A "1-year hold" means buying at one close and selling at the close 365 days later; fees and taxes aren't included. A start day only counts once its full holding period has passed, so the newest purchases are missing from the longer rows.

A 100% record describes the past. It reflects bitcoin's four-year rhythm so far and is not a promise about future periods.
